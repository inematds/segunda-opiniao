AI for people leaders

Monday Opinion

A weak manager uses AI to produce more. A strong manager uses AI to think better. 5 short lessons on using a chat AI as a mirror, advisor, simulator, and decision accelerator — never as a substitute for your leadership.

start now →

~20 min per session · nothing to install · works on mobile and desktop

What you’ll know how to do

Results, not theory

Make clear decisions — organize a real decision into options, risks, and blind spots before acting.

Prepare for difficult conversations — correct without humiliating, and recognize your own patterns as a leader.

Communicate without noise — clear messages and meetings that end with a decision, not a loose conversation.

Develop people — 30-day plans and change simulations before announcing anything.

Build the habit — a simple weekly ritual that sustains all this after the course ends.

“AI does not replace leadership. It increases the awareness of those who lead.”

Who it’s for

Made for where you are right now

it's for you if you

  • You lead a team at a small or midsize company and have never used AI for this.
  • You have little time and no technical background—you’ve never opened an AI chat before.
  • You want to make decisions, communicate, and develop people more clearly, not just “produce more.”

this isn’t for you if
